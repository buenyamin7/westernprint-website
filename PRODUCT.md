# westernprint.de

## Register
brand

## Product
Website and shop of westernprint GmbH, a small DTF/DTG textile print shop in Oberhausen, Germany, run by Bünyamin Dursun. Two jobs: (1) win print-on-demand shop owners as fulfillment partners (we print and ship their orders), (2) sell printed textiles to companies, clubs and individuals from 1 piece to large runs, including a self-service shop (Stanley/Stella and Stedman blanks, own motif upload, Mollie checkout).

## Target users
- POD shop owners (Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce), 20 to 40 years old, mobile-first, comparing fulfillment providers on speed, print quality and communication.
- Companies, clubs, schools and events ordering 10 to 500 printed pieces; want a quote within 24 hours.
- Individuals ordering single printed shirts in the shop.

## Purpose and desired outcome
Trust and speed. Visitors should feel "real workshop, real people, fast answers" and either request a quote (Angebot in 24h), order in the shop, or apply for a free sample. Every page ends in one clear action.

## Brand personality
Direct, warm, craft-proud, no agency fluff. Tone in German "du", short sentences. Visually dark, calm, premium, with one green accent (westernprint green). Photography is the hero: real workshop, real prints, real people. Reference: stoff3.de (structure, hero, confidence), not its copy.

## Anti-references
- Generic print-shop templates with clip-art shirts and price tables everywhere.
- Stock photography and AI-looking imagery; over-polished glossy renders.
- Loud discount-shop energy, red "SALE" badges, countdown timers.
- SaaS landing page clichés: gradient text, glass cards everywhere, eyebrows on every section.

## Constraints
- Zero running cost: static Astro site on GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Worker for checkout, Airtable as backend. No client-side frameworks.
- Dark theme default, light theme available via toggle; both must stay readable.
- German language, informal address ("du").
- Performance and SEO matter (local search "Textildruck Oberhausen", GEO for AI answers).

## Strategic design principles
1. Photos carry trust; layout stays out of their way. Big images, few decorations.
2. One accent color, used only for actions and highlights, never for decoration.
3. Every page has exactly one primary action, visible without scrolling on mobile.
4. Forms and checkout feel as calm as the marketing pages: same tokens, same spacing, inline validation with plain German messages.
5. Nothing that could be called a template: no identical card grids, no eyebrow on every section, no glass by default.

## Accessibility
Body text ≥ 4.5:1 on the dark background, focus rings visible, touch targets ≥ 44px, reduced-motion respected.
